About this site

Last updated: 13 September 2026

AI Coloring Page Generator is a small, free web tool that turns a photo into a black-and-white coloring page you can print. You drop in a picture — a pet, a family snapshot, a holiday photo — and it comes out as clean line art that is ready to colour in.

How it works

Everything happens in your browser. The photo is turned to grayscale, the tool looks for the edges in the picture, and those edges are drawn as black lines on white paper. You can adjust how much detail is kept, how thick the lines are, and whether the result is black-on-white or white-on-black.

When you are happy with the result, you can download it as a PNG or as a print-ready PDF at 300 DPI, in either A4 or US Letter size.

Why photos stay private

Your picture is never uploaded anywhere. There is no server-side processing and no image storage, so the photo you choose is only ever seen by your own browser. This also means the tool keeps working even if you are offline once the page has loaded.

Free, with no strings

No sign-up, no account, no watermark on your downloads, and no limit on how many pages you make. The site is supported by advertising.
