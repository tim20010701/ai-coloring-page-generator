Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This policy explains what happens with your information when you use AI Coloring Page Generator at photlin.com. It describes how the site actually works today.

Your photos never leave your browser

The conversion runs entirely inside your own browser. When you choose a photo, it is read into the page and processed there — the image is never uploaded to our server, and there is no server-side storage of your images. If you close the tab, the photo is gone. We cannot see, retrieve, or recover any picture you have processed.

What we do not collect

The site has no accounts, no sign-up, and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address, or any other personal details to use the tool. The site does not currently run analytics or visitor-tracking scripts.

Third-party advertising

This site displays third-party advertising. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, and they may collect information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you view. This is outside our control, and it is governed by the privacy policy of the advertising provider serving the ad. If you want to know which provider is currently serving ads on this site, contact us and we will tell you.

You can usually block or delete advertising cookies through your browser settings.

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Hosting

This site is a static site hosted on Cloudflare. Like any host, it handles the basic technical requests your browser makes when loading a page.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write in the message. We use it only to reply to you.

Children

This tool is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Parents and teachers can use it to make printable coloring pages from their own photos.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@photlin.com.
